## How to read this

- The eval set is stratified by keyword prior and symbol, not sampled by recency, so it deliberately includes ambiguous headlines. A number here will be lower than one measured on cherry-picked obvious cases.
- Bucket thresholds are `> +0.2` bullish, `< -0.2` bearish, neutral otherwise — the same rule the labels were written against.
- The majority-class baseline is the accuracy of always predicting the most common label. Beating 33% is not impressive; beating the majority-class row is the real floor.
- **50 of 50 labels are LLM-drafted, not human.** They were written by reading each headline and are a reasonable starting point, but until a human reviews them this is model-vs-model agreement, not ground truth. Flip `label_source` to `human` in `labeled_set.jsonl` as you confirm each row, then re-run.
- Article bodies here are Yahoo Finance summaries (~150 characters), not full text. Accuracy on full articles may differ.
